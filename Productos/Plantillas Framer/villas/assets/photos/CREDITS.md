> **Open: resale licence.** The Unsplash License allows free commercial use, but it does not allow selling the photos «as is» or redistributing them as a competing image collection. Shipping them inside a template that is sold has to be checked before launch; the safe default is to keep them as placeholders the buyer replaces, and to say so in the listing.

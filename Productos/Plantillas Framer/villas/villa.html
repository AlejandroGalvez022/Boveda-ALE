The house

Can Sal Blanca was a farmhouse for two hundred years before it became a family house. The walls are half a metre thick, so the rooms stay cool at midday without anyone touching the air conditioning.

Everything faces the pool and, past it, the sea and the rock of Es Vedrà. Breakfast happens under the pergola; dinner happens wherever the evening light is.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with minimal vanilla JS, no framework. Decided by the delivery path, not taste: the pages are imported into Framer with the official **HTML to Framer** Chrome extension, section by section, and then finished natively in Framer (breakpoints, CMS collection for villas, color and text styles). So layouts must be plain flex/grid that Framer can reproduce as stacks and frames. The signature background is the Lustre WebGL engine (`../../Haze/paquete`, `lustre.js`, zero dependencies); in Framer it becomes the Lustre code component (`framer.com/m/Lustre-3DWw9y.js`).

## Users

- **Buyer of the template:** owners and managers who rent out **luxury holiday villas with pools** by the night, and the designers or small studios who build sites for them in Framer. They buy a template to get a credible villa-rental site live fast, then edit it without code.
- **Visitor of the finished site:** travellers choosing a villa for a holiday. They compare villas, check what each one offers (sleeps, bedrooms, pool, distance to the beach), see photos, check rough price and dates, and ask to book.

## Product Purpose

A Framer template for **holiday villa rentals**, sold on the Framer Marketplace (planned at 69-79 $). It is the first test of a template business: success is measured in visits and sales over 4-6 weeks after launch; if it sells, more templates follow in other niches. For the end site, success is a guest sending a booking request.

## Positioning

The only template in the Marketplace's real-estate category built for **holiday villas with pools** (13-oct-2026: 27 real-estate templates, none for that; the closest is one generic rental template). Its signature is a **living pool-light background** — hand-written WebGL caustics from Lustre — that no other template has and free shader packs do not offer as a recognisable motif.

## Operating Context

- Bought and remixed on the Framer Marketplace; the live preview (a public Framer page in a 4:3 frame) and the listing images are the moment of decision.
- The buyer edits villas through a Framer CMS collection; listing and villa pages are generated from it.
- Booking happens by request (form or email) or through a link to an external booking engine. Which one the template leads with is **undecided**.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages v1: **Home, Villas (listing), Villa (detail), Contact / Booking**.
- Language: **English**. Demo content: **fictional Mediterranean villas** (Ibiza, Mallorca, Côte d'Azur and similar).
- Must stay reproducible in Framer: no layout tricks the HTML to Framer import or Framer's stacks cannot express.
- Responsive: desktop, tablet and phone (Framer breakpoints).
- Image licensing: photos must allow inclusion in a template that is sold. **Undecided**; to be checked before launch.
- Template name: **undecided**.

## Brand Commitments

- The Lustre **caustics** motif (pool light) is the template's signature.
- Everything else (name, palette, type) is open.

## Evidence on Hand

- Lustre engine, Framer component and promo images: `../../Haze/` (`paquete/`, `venta/portadas/`).
- No real clients, reviews, awards or booking data exist. Demo content is fictional sample content and must read as a believable example, never as a claim about real properties or real guests.

## Product Principles

1. **The villa sells the stay.** Photography and plain facts (sleeps, bedrooms, pool, beach, nights) lead; decoration follows.
2. **Every page leads to a booking request.** The way to ask is always one step away.
3. **Editable without code.** Anything a buyer will change lives in the CMS or in plain text and image layers.
4. **The water-light is a signature, not wallpaper.** Used where it means something, with restraint.
5. **The preview is the shop window.** The first viewport of the live preview has to win the sale on its own.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, including text over photos and over the moving background. Respect reduced motion (Lustre already shows a still frame). Keyboard and screen-reader friendly forms.

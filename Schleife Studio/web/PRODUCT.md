# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS written by hand, zero runtime dependencies, published on Netlify — the same way as La Brega Studio (`Ale/La Brega Studio/web/v5_DEF/`). Chosen by Alejandro on 2026-10-06 ("Como La Brega"). It must also open by double click (`file://`), so nothing may depend on `fetch()` of local files.

## Users

People in Gran Canaria who are thinking about getting a tattoo and are deciding who to trust with their skin. They arrive mostly from Instagram or from a link someone sends them, very often on a phone. Their job: see her real work, understand what she does (fine line, blackwork, Japanese sleeves, illustrative designs, cover-ups), feel that she is someone they can talk to, and write to her to book.

## Product Purpose

Give SCHLEIFE — Leonor's tattoo studio — a home outside Instagram that shows her work at the level it deserves. Success: more people writing to her by Instagram private message to book, with an idea of what they want.

The site is made by Alejandro, a friend of many years, as a gift. He wants the care to show: "le tengo mucho cariño y quiero que se vea reflejado". Quality bar: the La Brega Studio web, with no failures.

## Positioning

One artist, one hand: the same person draws the design and tattoos it. Her range goes from tiny fine-line pieces (initials, a sun and a moon for a mother and daughter) to full Japanese sleeves done over several sessions, plus her own illustrated designs ("Diseños"). Nothing on the site may claim more than that.

## Operating Context

- **Booking:** by Instagram private message to @schleifestudio (confirmed). The main call to action opens her Instagram.
- **Instagram is her hub:** @schleifestudio (name shown: SCHLEIFE; category "Artista"; bio: "@leonorglezzz cuenta personal · TattooStudio · Gran Canaria 📍"). Highlights: Tattoos, Ofertas, Diseños.
- **Where:** only "Gran Canaria" is public. No address on record.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page site: her work, what she does, her designs, who she is, how to book.
- Booking = link to Instagram DM. No form, no prices, no calendar.
- **Undecided / unknown (do not invent):** studio address and opening hours, prices, what the "Ofertas" are, aftercare text, minimum age/deposit policy, WhatsApp or email, her surname as displayed.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** SCHLEIFE · lockup "SCHLEIFESTUDIO · TATTOO ART". *Schleife* is German for bow / ribbon / loop.
- **Logo:** white monogram — an S drawn like a ribbon wrapped around a vertical needle — over a magenta field, with SCHLEIFESTUDIO in spaced serif capitals and TATTOO ART underneath. Only a 150 px raster exists (the Instagram avatar); an SVG reconstruction is provisional until she sends the original.
- **Artist:** Leonor (confirmed). Personal Instagram @leonorglezzz.
- **Voice (from her captions):** short, warm, emotional, with pink hearts — "Segunda sesión de esta manga 😭💕", "Fine line 🥰", "Cover 🩷", "Madre e hija!! ☀️🌙", "Algunos diseños 💕". Spanish, informal (tú).
- **Lettering:** her highlights use blackletter (Fraktur) — "Tattoos", "Ofertas", "Diseños".

## Product Principles

1. **Her work leads.** Every screen earns its place by showing her pieces better than Instagram does.
2. **Booking is one tap away**, always to her Instagram, never a dead end.
3. **Real only.** Her photos, her words, her name; anything unknown stays out rather than invented.
4. **Made with care.** The craft of the site is the gift: no broken states on any device, motion or no motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as on La Brega: readable contrast over photos, full keyboard use, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (the site must work and look finished without motion), works without JavaScript, 44 px touch targets.

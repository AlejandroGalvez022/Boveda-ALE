## Scope

One-page site for SCHLEIFE, Leonor's tattoo studio in Gran Canaria (`index.html`). Visitor mode: **Persuade** — the visitor should end up writing to her by Instagram private message.

## Audience, job, action

- People in Gran Canaria deciding who to trust with a tattoo; mostly on a phone, often arriving from Instagram or a friend's link.
- Job: see her real work, understand her range, feel she is approachable, write to her.
- Action: "Escríbeme por Instagram" → `https://ig.me/m/schleifestudio`, always reachable.
- Proof: her 19 real photos with her own captions and dates (`originales-instagram/`). No invented claims, prices, reviews, address or hours.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The S of her logo is a ribbon wound round a needle (*Schleife* = lazo, bow). The page IS that ribbon: it runs down through her work, flattens into a tattoo banner behind every section name, weaves behind her photos and ties itself into a bow over the Instagram button. It refuses the dark-studio default: grunge texture, masonry Instagram grid, neon accent, skull clip-art.

OWN-WORLD: Velvet black taken from her photo backdrop; her exact logo magenta #C50457 is the ribbon on black and on paper, and the ribbon turns white on the drenched magenta field, exactly like the logo. Ribbon front flat magenta; reverse shaded only with fine black hatching, never a derived tint. Paper white only where her drawings live. Fraktur for banner words (her highlights), spaced Roman capitals for the wordmark (her logo), a plain grotesk for everything practical. Focus = dotted stencil line; hover = dotwork fill; press = inverted magenta field.

STORY: A real piece of hers (the tiger), wrapped by the ribbon, says who she is in one look. Following the ribbon, the visitor sees ten pieces with their real caption, zone and date, her designs on paper, Leonor in her studio, three plain steps to book — and the bow ties on the button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: her tiger (a forearm on pure black, cut out from its own backdrop so the ribbon can pass behind it) stands right of centre, full height under the header, edges dissolving into the velvet; the needle hairline runs from just under the header to below the arm, its eye at the top; the thread leaves the eye, opens into the magenta ribbon and winds round the arm; the flat front panel crosses the arm at ~60% height carrying SCHLEIFE in Roman capitals. Left: "Tatuajes de / Leonor (Fraktur) / en Gran Canaria", one line of range, the "Escríbeme por Instagram" button. Header: monogram + SCHLEIFE left, four anchors, "Escríbeme" right. Phone: the arm on top (up to 56svh, never wider than the screen), copy and button below, the button inside the first screen from 320×640 up.

FORM: Traditional tattoo banner fused with her own monogram — candidate 6 of 7 on my ordered list; seed key 7ddb5254. Signature interaction: the ribbon unwinds along its length as you scroll and ties the bow at the end. Raises kept from the declined hand: states change technique not tint (Memphis); billboard-to-footnote scale (Greiman); practical copy in a plain register (sleeping city); real facts in tabular figures (forge); one strict state vocabulary with dotted focus (one-bit); one magenta lead per viewport (nixie).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
